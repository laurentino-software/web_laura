Cookie Policy

On my website, we use cookies to improve your user experience and facilitate appointment bookings. Below we explain what cookies are, which ones we use, and how you can manage them.

1. What is a cookie?

A cookie is a small text file that is downloaded to your device (computer, smartphone, tablet) when accessing certain web pages. They allow a web page, among other things, to store and retrieve information about the browsing habits of a user or their equipment.

2. What types of cookies does this website use?

This website uses the following types of cookies:

  • Technical cookies: These are necessary for the website to function. In our case, they are used to remember your privacy preferences (whether you have accepted cookies or not).
  • Third-party cookies (Doctoralia): When loading the Doctoralia appointment booking widget, third-party cookies managed by Docplanner (Doctoralia) are downloaded. These cookies can be:
    • Analytical: To measure the performance and use of the widget.
    • Tracking/Advertising: They may be used to track user activity across different websites to display relevant advertising.

3. Consent and Blocking

In compliance with current regulations (GDPR), the appointment booking widget is blocked by default, and no third-party cookies will be downloaded until you expressly accept the use of cookies in the informational banner on the main page.

4. How to manage cookies

You can allow, block, or delete cookies installed on your equipment through your browser settings:

  • Google Chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Safari
  • Microsoft Edge
