Laura Rebollo, psychologist in Alcalá de Henares

I am Laura Rebollo, a licensed clinical psychologist (M-14767) offering both online and in-person sessions in Alcalá de Henares while collaborating with Kaizen Psicología y Formación and Kairós EMCE. I treat adults and couples in Spanish and English.

During my career I have supported people with intellectual disabilities, delivered workshops for children and teens, and guided individuals and couples through therapy. Lifelong learning is essential to keeping the care I provide effective and compassionate.

This space is here to help you make sense of what you are experiencing and to build tools so you can move toward the change you want.

My specialties in psychology

Here are some of the areas where I can help you.

Starting therapy does not mean something is wrong; it means choosing to care for your emotional wellbeing.

When we cannot change a situation, the challenge is to change ourselves.

Act as if what you do makes a difference. Because it does.

The curious paradox is that when I accept myself as I am, then I can change.
