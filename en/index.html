Psychological Specialities

I accompany people going through times when emotions are heavy, self-confidence suffers or pain takes up too much space.

My intention is to offer a safe place where you can understand what is happening to you, feel supported and find a kinder path towards your well-being.

Emotional Regulation

Includes learning to identify what you feel and responding in a more balanced way. It allows you to understand your reactions, reduce the intensity of distress and regain a sense of internal control.

Self-esteem

It is the way you value and treat yourself. When strengthened, it improves your security, your decisions and your well-being. Working on it involves recognizing your resources, your limits and your own value.

Depression and Grief

They can generate emotional exhaustion, confusion and a sense of disconnection. Understanding what is happening and receiving support makes it easier to go through the pain and find a path of relief and reconstruction.
